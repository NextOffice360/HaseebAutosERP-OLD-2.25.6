   - Prevent duplicate entries, validate postal codes, suggest corrections.

7. **Geofenced Label Printing**
   - Filter members by city/area and auto-generate labels grouped by geography for efficient logistics.

## 💡 GENERAL UI/UX IMPROVEMENTS (APPLICABLE TO ALL SCREENS)

### 1. **Consistent Layout & Visual Hierarchy**
- Use consistent spacing, typography, and color coding.
- Highlight primary actions (e.g., “Generate”, “Print”) with accent colors.

### 2. **Progressive Disclosure**
- Hide advanced options (like credit info) behind “Advanced Settings” toggle.
- Reduce cognitive load for new users.

### 3. **Inline Validation & Tooltips**
- Show real-time feedback on invalid inputs (dates, numbers).
- Add tooltips explaining terms like “Don’t Care”, “Near Matching Value”.

### 4. **Contextual Help & Onboarding**
- Add “?” icons next to complex fields.
- Provide guided tour for first-time users.

### 5. **Keyboard Shortcuts & Accessibility**
- Support tab navigation, Enter key for search, Ctrl+P for print.
- Ensure WCAG compliance for visually impaired users.

### 6. **Responsive Design Consideration**
- Even if desktop-only now, design components to scale for future tablet/mobile use.

### 7. **Undo/Redo & Transaction History**
- Allow reversal of accidental block/delete actions.
- Keep log of every change with timestamp and user ID.
